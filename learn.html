Chapter 01
The Language of the Cube
Every algorithm is written in standard WCA notation. Think of the cube as having 6 faces. The letter tells you which face to turn. The modifier tells you how to turn it.
The 3 Rules

Letter Only (R): Turn that face 90° Clockwise.
Apostrophe (R'): Pronounced "Prime". Turn 90° Anti-Clockwise.
Number 2 (R2): Turn that face 180° (Double turn). Direction doesn't matter.

Chapter 02
The White Cross
The foundation of the solve. You need to form a White Cross on the bottom face. But there is a strict rule: The side colors of your white edges MUST match the center pieces of the adjacent faces.
1

The Beginner "Daisy" Method

If building directly on the bottom is confusing, build it on top first. Move all 4 White edges to surround the Yellow center piece. Orientation of the side colors doesn't matter yet.

2

Match and Drop

Look at the side color of one of your top white edges (e.g., Red). Rotate the top layer (U) until that Red edge lines up over the Red center piece. Once matched, turn that front face twice (F2) to drop it to the bottom. Repeat 3 more times.

Chapter 03
First Two Layers (F2L)
Forget solving corner-by-corner and edge-by-edge. F2L combines them. You find a matching Corner (has white) and Edge (no white), pair them up in the top layer, and insert them into their slot simultaneously.
The Golden Rule of F2L

If the white sticker is pointing UP, you match the edge to its center color. If the white sticker is on the SIDE, you look at the top colors of the two pieces to decide how to pair them.

The 3 Basic Scenarios
A

Top Colors are Different

When the corner and edge are on top, and their top-facing colors are different (e.g., Corner is Green, Edge is Red). You simply bring them together so they connect smoothly, then insert using R U' R'.

B

Top Colors are the Same

If both top colors match (e.g., both are Red), you need to "Hide" the corner in the bottom layer, move the edge away, and bring the corner back up so they form a pair separated by one block. Insert with R U R'.

C

White is Pointing Up

Match the edge piece with its center color. Turn that face away from you, rotate the top layer so the corner drops directly on top of the edge, then pull them both back up. The pair is made.

Chapter 04
Orient Last Layer (OLL)
Make the entire top face Yellow. We will use the 2-Look method. First, make a yellow cross (Step 1). Then, orient the yellow corners (Step 2).
How to read these diagrams

You are looking straight down at the top of the 3x3 grid. The thin bars on the outside edges represent Yellow stickers that are facing sideways.

Step 1: Yellow Edges (Cross)

Horizontal Line
F R U R' U' F'
Make sure the yellow line is horizontal across the center before executing.
Angle (L-Shape)
f R U R' U' f'
Hold the yellow edges at 9 o'clock and 12 o'clock (Left and Back).

Step 2: Yellow Corners

Sune (Case 27)
R U R' U R U2 R'
One corner is yellow. Hold it in the Bottom-Left.
Anti-Sune (Case 26)
R U2 R' U' R U' R'
One corner is yellow. Hold it in the Bottom-Right.
H / Cross (Case 21)
F R U R' U' R U R' U' R U R' U' F'
No corners yellow. Headlights facing front AND back.
Chapter 05
Permute Last Layer (PLL)
The top is completely Yellow. Now we move the pieces around to finish the cube. Step 1 swaps the corners. Step 2 swaps the edges.

Step 1: Corners

Adjacent Swap (T Perm)
R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' R U R' F'
Look for "Headlights" (two corners same color on one side). Hold them on the LEFT.
Diagonal Swap (Y Perm)
F R U' R' U' R U R' F' R U R' U' R' F R F'
No headlights anywhere. Swaps diagonal corners.

Step 2: Edges

Ua Perm (Clockwise)
M2 U M U2 M' U M2
One solid bar is solved. Hold it at the BACK. Cycles 3 front edges clockwise.
H Perm (Opposites)
M2 U M2 U2 M2 U M2
No solid bars. Every edge needs to go to the opposite side.
