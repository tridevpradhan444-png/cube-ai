Chapter 01
Notation
Before you learn any algorithm, you need to understand the language of cubing. Every move has a letter code. Once you know these, any algorithm in the world makes sense.
💡 Key Concept

A letter = clockwise turn of that face when looking directly at it.
A letter + ' (prime/apostrophe) = anticlockwise.
A letter + 2 = two turns (same as turning it twice, direction doesn't matter).

How to hold the cube: White on the bottom. Yellow on top. Green facing you. This is the standard orientation.

Prime & Double

The apostrophe means anticlockwise turn.

Turn it twice. Same result either way.

Slice Moves
M, S, E Slices
Slice moves turn the middle layer instead of an outer face.
M
Middle Slice
The middle layer between L and R.
S
Standing Slice
The middle layer between F and B.
E
Equatorial Slice
The middle layer between U and D.
r
Wide Move
Lowercase = R + M together (two layers).
📌 Quick Reference

R U R' U' — "Sexy move"
F R U R' U' F' — OLL edge orientation
R U R' U R U2 R' — Sune (important OLL)

Most algorithms are combinations of these patterns.

Chapter 02
The Cross
The cross is the first step of CFOP. You need to form a white "+" on the bottom face, with each edge matching the center of its side face.
Target State
White Cross
Bottom face with white cross pattern.
Alignment
Side Must Align
Each edge must match its center color.
Beginner Method
The Daisy Method
The easiest way to learn cross. Make a "daisy" on top, then flip edges down.
1
Make a Daisy on Top
Find all 4 white edges. Move them to the top face (U face), white side facing up.
2
Align Each Edge
Rotate top until edge is above its correct slot.
3
Flip It Down — F2
Do F2 to flip the white edge to the bottom.
4
Repeat for All 4 Edges
Repeat steps 2-3 for the remaining 3 edges.
Efficient Cross
Solving Cross in 8 Moves or Less
Once you can do daisy cross, aim for efficiency.
1
Use Inspection Time
WCA gives you 15 seconds. Plan your entire cross.
2
Work in Consistent Order
Always solve cross edges in the same order (e.g., green → orange → blue → red).
3
Solve Pairs Together
Sometimes two edges can be solved with one sequence.
Chapter 03
F2L — First Two Layers
F2L means solving the first two layers by pairing corner-edge pieces. Mastering F2L will speed up your solves significantly.
What is a pair?

Each F2L slot needs a corner (3 colors, including white) and an edge (2 colors, no white).

Intuitive F2L
How to Think Through F2L
You don't need to memorize all 41 cases. Understand the goal: pair corner and edge in U layer, then insert.
1
Find Your Pieces
Find the corner and edge you need for each slot.
2
Bring Both to Top
Extract pieces to the U layer if needed.
3
Pair Them Up
Use U moves to connect corner and edge.
4
Insert the Pair
Use R U' R' or L' U L to insert smoothly.
💡 Lookahead

While inserting one pair, look for the next one. Your hands should run on autopilot.

Core Algorithms
Most Important F2L Cases

Sexy Move (Right)
R U R' U'
Most common F2L trigger. Learn as one fluid motion.
Anti-Sexy (Left)
L' U' L U
Mirror of sexy move.
Chapter 04
OLL — Orient Last Layer
OLL means making the entire top face yellow. After F2L, you'll have some yellow stickers but not all. One algorithm orients every piece at once.
💡 Start with 2-Look OLL

Don't learn all 57 cases at once. 2-Look OLL uses just 7 algorithms. Later learn the full 57.

Key Cases
Essential OLL Algorithms
Sune 2-Look
R U R' U R U2 R'
Most important OLL. One yellow corner facing up on left.
Horizontal Line 2-Look
F R U R' U' F'
Yellow line going left-right.
Dot 2-Look
F R U R' U' F' f R U R' U' f'
Only center yellow. No edges oriented.
Chapter 05
PLL — Permute Last Layer
PLL means putting all the yellow pieces in their correct positions. The top face is already all yellow from OLL.
Goal

All 8 corner and edge pieces on top layer go to their correct slots. Position matters, not orientation.

Learning Path
Start with 2-Look PLL
2-Look PLL uses just 2 algorithms:
T Perm (Corners)
R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' R U R' F'
Permute the 3 top corners.
M2 U M2 U2 M2 U M2 (Edges)
M2 U M2 U2 M2 U M2
Cycle the 4 top edges.
Chapter 06
2-Look CFOP Summary
A beginner-friendly method using just 10 algorithms total. Master these and you can solve any scramble!
The 10 Algorithms
Complete 2-Look Repertoire
1
Cross (White)
Layer-by-layer. Use intuition + flip moves (F2, D', R, D).
2-5
F2L (4 slots)
Pair + insert. Main algorithms: R U R' U', L' U' L U, Sexy move variations.
6
OLL (Yellow Edges)
3 cases: Dot (F R U R' U' F' repeat), Line (F R U R' U' F'), or Sune (R U R' U R U2 R').
7
OLL (Corners)
Usually done by Sune or its mirrors. Apply until all corners face up.
8
PLL (Corners)
T-Perm or Yz-Perm. Moves all top corners to correct slots.
9
PLL (Edges)
M2 U M2 U2 M2 U M2 or H-Perm. Finishes the cube.
Tips for 2-Look Success

✓ Slow down first. Focus on understanding, not speed.
✓ Practice each step separately. Don't rush to whole solves.
✓ Drill rotations. Once you know the algorithms, drill them 50+ times each.
✓ Lookahead matters. While solving one step, look for the next pieces.
✓ Use the practice tool in this app to get scrambles for each stage.

Next Steps
Once you're comfortable with 2-Look CFOP (sub-2 minutes), consider:
  • Full OLL (57 cases) — Skip 2-look edge orientation step
  • Full PLL (21 cases) — Skip 2-look corner/edge permutation
  • Better cross — Solve directly without daisy method
  • Faster F2L — Improve lookahead and rotationless solutions
  • Better finger tricks — Smooth, fluid hand movements
